📍 Case Status Journey — how a case moves through the system
Lead → Pending → In Progress → Submitted → Decision → Approved ✓  or  Rejected ✗
Any case can also move to Closed at any stage. A closed case can be re-opened by setting it back to "In Progress".

⚠️ 30-day portal rule: If a client's last case is closed, they automatically lose access to their portal after 30 days. This happens automatically — no action needed from you.
