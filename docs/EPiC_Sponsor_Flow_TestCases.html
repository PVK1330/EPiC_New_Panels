  3. Submit

  1. Enter a wrong password

F. Payment Confirmation & Receipt 6 cases

The sponsor pays the UKVI licence fee on the UKVI portal, then confirms payment in the app — optionally attaching the receipt. Only the sponsor can confirm their own payment.

IDTest casePre-conditionsStepsExpected resultResult
TC-F1 "Confirm Payment" card appears Application at Decision Pending; payment not confirmed
  1. Open Licence Tracking → Status
 A Confirm Payment card is shown at the top with an optional Upload receipt control and an I have confirmed payment button. Pass
TC-F2 Confirm payment without a receipt On the Confirm Payment card
  1. Click I have confirmed payment (no file)
 Payment is recorded with a timestamp; a success message is shown; the case team is notified. Pass
TC-F3 Confirm payment with an optional receipt On the Confirm Payment card
  1. Click Upload receipt (optional) and choose a PDF/image
  2. Click I have confirmed payment
 Payment is confirmed and the receipt is stored; success message notes the receipt was attached. Pass
TC-F4 View the uploaded receipt Payment confirmed with a receipt (TC-F3)
  1. On the confirmed card, click View uploaded receipt
 The receipt opens in a new tab; the file is served securely (no public path exposed). Pass
TC-F5 Staff cannot confirm the sponsor's payment Logged in as Admin or Caseworker on the application's stages
  1. Open the Application Stages
  2. Locate the sponsor's payment-confirmation task
 There is no "Mark complete" control for the sponsor payment task; attempting it server-side is rejected. Only the sponsor can confirm payment. Pass
TC-F6 Mistaken staff completion auto-reverts The sponsor payment task was completed by a non-sponsor while payment is not actually confirmed
  1. Reload the application stages
 The payment task automatically returns to pending so the sponsor can confirm; a genuine sponsor confirmation is never reverted. Pass
